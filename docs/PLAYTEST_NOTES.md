# Playtest notes — vertical slice (M5)

Goal: would you happily show **Lapland Fell** to friends? Tune `content/balance.tres` and the
trail data from what you see here, not from what testers say they'd prefer.

## How to run a session (10–15 min per person)
1. Fresh install (or clear app data) so they start at the trail select with only trail 1 open.
2. Hand them the phone, say only: *"It's a hiking game. Get to the summit."* Don't explain controls.
3. Watch quietly. Note where they hesitate, what they tap, what makes them smile or sigh.
4. Let them play Forest Lake Loop → Archipelago Rocks → Lapland Fell.
5. Afterwards, ask the questions below.

## Watch for
- Did they find **hold to walk / tap to jump** without help? How long did it take?
- Did they **rest on purpose** before running out, or only after "Exhausted"?
- Did they **use benches**, and notice the ×2?
- Did they **try photos**? Did a missed shot feel unfair?
- Gusts on Archipelago: did they figure out that **standing still braces** them?
- Fog on Lapland Fell: atmospheric, or annoying?
- Any moment they looked lost, bored or frustrated?
- Did any trail end **after dusk**? (= daylight too short, or they lingered on purpose?)

## Ask afterwards
1. What was the best moment? The worst?
2. Was anything confusing?
3. Did the hike feel too short, too long, or about right?
4. Would you play a new trail next week? Why / why not?
