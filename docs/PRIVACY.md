# Peak Rush — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-09_

Peak Rush is a single-player offline game. This policy covers version 1 of the game
on Android and iOS.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** Peak Rush does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No in-app purchases.
- The game does not use the network, camera, microphone, location or contacts.
  The in-game "camera" only takes pictures of the game world; nothing is saved to your photo library.

## What stays on your device
Your progress (completed trails, stars, best times, journal photos, gear and settings) is saved
in a file inside the app's private storage on your device. It never leaves the device
except through your phone's own backup feature, if you have turned that on (that backup is
handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies).

Uninstalling the game deletes this file.

## Children
Peak Rush collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If a future version adds a feature that handles data (for example cloud saves), this policy
will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
